From exp((zv+εv)/τ) with ε ~ N(0, σq2) iid: the log-odds between two tokens shifts by (εv−εw)/τ, standard deviation √2·σq/τ. The paper does not give σq, so the axis is in units of σq.

Code graph and the dilution bound (§3.1)

The repository is a typed graph Grepo = (V, E, R, X) over six relations: CALLS, CALLED_BY, IMPORTS, INHERITS, PARALLEL_SYNC_ASYNC, DOCS_EXAMPLE. At turn t the active tokens split into A (issue contract plus 1-hop slice) and Bt (accumulated tracebacks and rejected diffs). Proposition 1, adapted from Lemma 39 of Sanford et al.:

Attnh(X)N = [ Zh,A·exp(Lh,A) + Zh,Bt·exp(Lh,Bt) ] / [ exp(Lh,A) + exp(Lh,Bt) ]

As |Bt| grows, exp(Lh,Bt) dominates the denominator. The paper states the qualitative effect and one measured pair (dilution 0.72 → 0.06 after G-CCR, §3.1) but no closed form for Lh,Bt(|Bt|). The simulator uses the uniform-logit reading stated under its chart.
